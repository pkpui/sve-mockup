Dassault Falcon 2000

One of the most successful and demanded Falcons ever built. Transcontinental range, wide-body comfort, and ultra-cost efficiency — purpose-built for US coast-to-coast and Eastern Pacific flights.

Falcon 2000 N42ST

More than 300 Falcon 2000 are in service today. This plane was built with United States transcontinental and Eastern Pacific flights in mind. Its wide spacious cabin and large baggage area as well as a full galley area make your experience second to none.

Along with the Falcon 2000's range and speed, it is one of the most sought-after aircraft in business jet travel. Whether a quick business trip to Chicago or a well-deserved vacation to the Hawaiian Islands, the Falcon 2000 will take you in luxury and safety.
