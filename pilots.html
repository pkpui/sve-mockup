Our Pilots

Hand-Selected. Falcon-Rated. Annually Trained.

Every SVE pilot is hand-selected for experience, professionalism, and temperament. Your crew isn't a random assignment — they know the aircraft, they know the routes, and they know our standards.

Crew Standards

Silicon Valley Express will handle all crew staffing coordination as well as scheduling of training at any level that you require. With pre-existing flight crews, we will complete additional training in our policies and procedures as to ensure a positive, safe working environment.

Annual or biennial flight training or checks will be done at either CAE or FlightSafety International, in addition SVE will coordinate all FAA required emergency training for both 91 and 135 operations.

What This Means for You

When you step aboard the Falcon 2000, the crew greeting you has been through rigorous selection, type-specific training on full-motion simulators, and ongoing proficiency checks that exceed FAA minimums. They've flown these routes hundreds of times. They know the FBOs, the approach procedures, and the weather patterns. That's the SVE standard.

Training Program

How We Train Our Pilots

Simulator Training

Annual recurrent training at CAE or FlightSafety International on full-motion Falcon 2000 simulators. Pilots practice emergency procedures, system failures, and adverse weather scenarios.

Emergency Procedures

All FAA-required emergency training for both Part 91 and Part 135 operations, coordinated by SVE. Evacuation, fire suppression, first aid, and crew resource management.

SVE Standards

Additional training in SVE policies and procedures beyond FAA minimums. Our standards exceed the industry in every category — because your safety is non-negotiable.

Crew Selection

Every SVE pilot is hand-selected for experience, professionalism, and temperament. We don't just hire pilots — we select crew members who represent the SVE standard of excellence.
