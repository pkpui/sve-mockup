Safety Standards

Safety Is Paramount

Our impeccable safety background begins with the highest standards of manufacturer and FAA inspections, paired with our highly seasoned maintenance technicians and hand-selected pilots.

Our Safety Framework

Safety Management System is part of our culture. SVE, in partnership with CAT maintenance, mitigates risk at every level — this is how we have achieved the highest echelon of safety for over three decades.

⚑

FAA Part 135 Certification

Silicon Valley Express holds an FAA Part 135 Air Carrier Certificate — the federal standard for commercial charter operations. This means our operations, maintenance, crew training, and dispatch procedures are subject to regular FAA oversight and inspection.

🛡

Safety Management System (SMS)

Our SMS program is integrated into every operation. Risk identification, hazard reporting, and continuous improvement are not checklists — they are part of our culture. Every team member is empowered to flag safety concerns without hesitation.

✈

Pilot Training — CAE & FlightSafety

Annual or biennial flight training and checks are completed at either CAE or FlightSafety International on full-motion Falcon 2000 simulators. SVE coordinates all FAA-required emergency training for both Part 91 and Part 135 operations. Our pilots exceed FAA minimums.

🔧

Maintenance — Corporate Air Technology

In close coordination with Corporate Air Technology (CAT), SVE ensures your mission operates at the highest level. All pricing includes genuine manufacturer parts and all labor. Heavy maintenance is arranged with the aircraft manufacturer or major service centers and monitored by SVE.

★

BBB A+ Accreditation

Silicon Valley Express has maintained an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau since 2009 — reflecting our commitment to transparency, accountability, and client satisfaction across every aspect of our operations.

🏠

30+ Year Safety Record

Operating since the 1980s from San Jose International Airport, SVE has maintained an impeccable safety record. This isn't a marketing claim — it's the result of three decades of disciplined operations, conservative decision-making, and investment in training and maintenance.

Training Standards

How We Train Our Pilots

Simulator Training

Annual recurrent training at CAE or FlightSafety International on full-motion Falcon 2000 simulators. Pilots practice emergency procedures, system failures, and adverse weather scenarios in a controlled environment.

Emergency Procedures

All FAA-required emergency training for both Part 91 and Part 135 operations, coordinated by SVE. This includes evacuation procedures, fire suppression, first aid, and crew resource management.

SVE Standards

With pre-existing flight crews, we complete additional training in our policies and procedures to ensure a positive, safe working environment. Our standards exceed FAA minimums in every category.

Crew Selection

Every SVE pilot is hand-selected for experience, professionalism, and temperament. We don't just hire pilots — we select crew members who represent the SVE standard of excellence.

Questions About Safety?

We're Happy to Discuss Our Standards

Safety transparency is a core value. Ask us anything about our certifications, training programs, or maintenance procedures.
