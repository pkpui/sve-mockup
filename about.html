Our Story

Silicon Valley Express (SVE) Aircraft Management has been part of Silicon Valley since the beginning — over three decades of diverse aircraft management and charter. A truly unique company, SVE has intentionally remained a small company to bring our clients the highest level of personalized service.

Resources and relationships nurtured over these years have enabled SVE to pass on better-than-fleet savings to our clients.

Silicon Valley Express professional team can manage one of your most valuable assets 24/7, 365 days a year from our base at Mineta San Jose International Airport or anywhere in the world. We have a diverse background in all aspects of aircraft management of Part 91 and Part 135 operations with all types of aircraft.

We have an impeccable safety record that starts with our SMS (Safety Management System) along with our highly qualified, hand-selected pilots and a close relationship with Corporate Air Technology Aircraft Maintenance.

Our Base

Silicon Valley Express began operations in the 1980s, when San Jose Jet Center was first built. With this long history, our base today remains with Atlantic Aviation. With this great relationship we can pass on our great hangar and fuel discounts to lower your yearly operation costs. SVE is a premier base tenant of Atlantic Aviation.
