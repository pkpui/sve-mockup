Charter Services

Levels Beyond First Class

Whether a simple day trip, an extensive business trip, or a well-deserved vacation — our one-on-one inflight experience is tailored to your every need. Our personalized concierge service will go beyond your expectations to destinations across the globe.

Your Personal Flight Department

Silicon Valley Express maintains 24-hour scheduling and dispatch. All trips are handled by our dispatch department and coordinated with the aircraft owner or designated personnel. Information concerning flight times, airports, FBOs, weather, catering, ground transportation, hotels, and airlines will be available through our dispatch office.

It will be as if it is your personal flight department or concierge. We will cover all your aviation needs from start to finish. Our nationwide contacts and years of service will be passed on to our clients. Extreme care and dedication by our team ensures every mission is completed to exceed your expectations.

The Charter Experience

For Business

Your airborne office, with Wi-Fi, FlightPhone, widebody quiet cabin. Work, conference, or simply prepare for your meeting in complete privacy at 47,000 feet.

For Pleasure
