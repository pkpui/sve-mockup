Charter Questions Answered

Charter rates from San Jose (SJC) vary by aircraft type, destination, and trip duration. Our Falcon 2000 typically ranges from $4,500-$6,000 per flight hour. A round trip to Los Angeles averages $18,000-$24,000, while Hawaii runs $40,000-$65,000. Contact us for a personalized quote — we respond within 30 minutes.

A charter operator like Silicon Valley Express directly owns or manages the aircraft and employs the pilots. A broker is a middleman who arranges flights on other operators' aircraft, typically adding a 10-15% commission. When you fly with SVE, you deal directly with the operator — no broker markup, full transparency on safety standards, and direct accountability.

Silicon Valley Express holds an FAA Part 135 Air Carrier Certificate and maintains a comprehensive Safety Management System (SMS). All pilots complete annual training at CAE or FlightSafety International, and our maintenance is performed in partnership with Corporate Air Technology (CAT) using genuine manufacturer parts. We have maintained an impeccable safety record for over 30 years.

Our primary charter aircraft is the Dassault Falcon 2000 (N42ST), a large-cabin, long-range jet seating up to 8 passengers. With a range of 3,000 nautical miles and speeds up to Mach 0.85, it handles transcontinental flights and Hawaii with ease. We also have access to a Hawker 800XP for shorter missions.

We recommend booking 48-72 hours in advance for domestic flights, though we can accommodate same-day requests when the aircraft is available. International flights typically require 5-7 days for permits and customs arrangements. Our 24-hour dispatch team is always available at (408) 292-0677.
