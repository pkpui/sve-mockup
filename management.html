Aircraft Management

Managing Your Most Valuable Asset

Over thirty years of professional, personalized service that is second to none. Silicon Valley Express has been there since the beginning to meet the demand of executive transportation and management.

Full-Service Flight Department

Entering into a management agreement with Silicon Valley Express will bring immediate utilization of a full-service flight department or an "a la carte" program tailored to your company's needs.

In the agreement, SVE will manage all aspects of aircraft operations and maintenance, including training, flight scheduling, 24-hour flight following, maintenance coordination, as well as accounting.

Each month you will receive a detailed management statement of all aircraft activity, costs and expenses. Our team will work with you one-on-one to answer any questions and strategize the most cost effective, economical approach to operate your aircraft with the highest-level safety, all above industry standards. All reports will include copies of all invoices related to your aircraft operation.

We have various levels of service to meet all your aviation needs. Let us tailor a program that fits your flight department for many years to come.

Hangar Storage

SVE began operations in the 1980s when San Jose Jet Center was first built. Our base today remains with Atlantic Aviation. With this great relationship we pass on our great hangar and fuel discounts to lower your yearly operation costs. SVE is a premier base tenant of Atlantic Aviation.

Insurance

SVE's excellent safety record and highly trained crews enable us to pass on the very best comprehensive insurance rates to our clients, helping lower overall yearly budgets. Additional coverage can be arranged quickly for international operations.

Fuel Programs

SVE lowers your operating costs with unbeatable base and nationwide fuel prices arranged through vendors we have negotiated and worked closely with for the past three decades. We pride ourselves in negotiating the lowest possible fuel prices and passing on the savings.

Crew Staffing

SVE handles all crew staffing coordination as well as scheduling of training. With pre-existing flight crews, we complete additional training in our policies and procedures. Annual training at CAE or FlightSafety International.

Maintenance Coordination

In partnership with Corporate Air Technology (CAT), SVE coordinates all scheduled and unscheduled maintenance. Heavy maintenance arranged with aircraft manufacturer or major service centers, monitored by SVE.

Financial Reporting

Detailed monthly management statements of all aircraft activity, costs, and expenses. All reports include copies of all invoices. Full transparency into your aircraft's operating costs.
